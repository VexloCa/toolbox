# Competitor Radar

> A monthly competitor briefing from what they publish publicly

**Category:** Strategy & Planning · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

You check competitors twice a year: once in a panic after losing a deal, once while updating the website. Both times you skim their site, feel vaguely worried, and retain nothing. There is no record of what their pricing said last quarter, so when it changes you don't notice, and when a prospect quotes a competitor's new offer at you, it is the first you've heard of it.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. Answer the setup questions about your business and the 2 to 5 competitors worth tracking.
4. Do a first paste for each competitor: pricing page, main service pages, and recent posts. This becomes the baseline.
5. Set a monthly reminder. Fifteen minutes of copy-paste, then read the briefing.
6. Put the "what to do about it" actions straight into your task list. The briefing is an input; the actions are the output.

## Example

Say you run a managed IT firm in Calgary tracking three local rivals. In month two you paste refreshed pages. Briefing (condensed): "Change log: Competitor A's pricing page now leads with a per-user price ($45/user) instead of a flat tier; their old setup fee is gone (as pasted July 3 vs June 5). Competitor B published two posts targeting law firms, a vertical you also serve. Competitor C: no material pasted this month, status UNKNOWN. Positioning: A is moving down-market on price; your 'senior techs only' angle is now the clearest differentiator on the map. Do about it: 1) Add a per-user comparison row to your pricing FAQ, owner: you, by July 18. 2) Ask your two law-firm clients for a referral or testimonial before B's content gains ground, owner: you, by July 25."

## Pro tip

Paste your own pricing and service pages too, and refresh them when they change. The positioning map is twice as useful when your side of it is current, and the skill can flag when a competitor's change makes your published copy sound dated.
