# Review & Reputation Manager

> More reviews coming in and every one answered well

**Category:** Customer Support · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

Your happiest customers walk out the door and never write the review, because nobody asked at the right moment. Meanwhile the one angry review from March sits unanswered at the top of your Google profile, doing quiet damage to every prospect who checks. Asking feels awkward, replying to criticism in public feels risky, so both jobs get skipped. Which is how a good business ends up with a 3.9 and silence.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. Answer the setup questions about your platforms, voice, and when a job counts as "done."
4. Ask for your review-request sequence and load the drafts into however you message customers.
5. As reviews arrive, paste each one for a reply draft. Send it after a human read-through.
6. Monthly, paste everything new and read the summary. The fix suggestions are where the money is.

## Example

Say you run a two-van appliance repair company. Setup learns that jobs close same-day and customers are happiest right after the fix works. The skill drafts a two-touch sequence: a text 3 hours after job completion ("while the dishwasher's running its first load") and one email follow-up four days later, then stop. A 2-star review arrives: "Tech was late, no call." The reply draft acknowledges the missed window, names the fix ("we've added an on-the-way text to every booking"), and offers a direct contact, 61 words, no excuses. The monthly summary counts three lateness mentions in June against zero in May and suggests the real action: look at Friday scheduling, not the wording of the apology.

## Pro tip

Paste your existing reviews, all of them, during setup. The skill learns which specifics customers already praise (the callback speed, the tidy work) and starts weaving those exact phrases into your ask sequences, which reads as authentic because it is.
