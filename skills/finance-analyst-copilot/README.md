# Finance Analyst Copilot

> Understand your monthly numbers and cash position in plain English

**Category:** Finance & Admin · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

Most small business owners see their numbers once a month, in a report they don't fully read, from software they don't fully trust themselves to interpret. Cash surprises arrive with two weeks' notice instead of eight, prices go years without a review, and overdue invoices sit unchased because writing a firm-but-polite reminder is awkward. The information exists. The plain-English understanding doesn't.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. Answer the skill's setup questions about your business.
4. Set a monthly ritual: export your P&L and current balances, paste them in, and read the review before your accountant meeting: strip out any account numbers first.

## Example

A cafe owner pastes June's P&L export and her bank balance. Output (condensed): "The one-line summary: June revenue was up 8% on May, but you kept less of it: margin slipped from 62% to 57% (margin: the share of each sales dollar left after ingredient costs). Top expense movements: dairy up $410: likely the supplier change you mentioned; a second POS subscription appeared ($89/mo): possible duplicate; one-time patio repair $600. Watch this: your 8-week cash view dips to $2,100 in week 5 when quarterly insurance hits. Ask your accountant: whether the patio repair can be treated differently for tax. I'm not a licensed financial advisor or accountant: please verify anything important with your accountant before acting on it."

## Pro tip

Paste two or three months of history in your first session, not just the current month. Trends are where this skill earns its keep, and it can only spot a creeping cost if it has seen the baseline.
