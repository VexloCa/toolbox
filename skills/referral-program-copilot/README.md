# Referral Program Copilot

> Referrals become a system with a memory, not a thing you remember to do twice a year

**Category:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT

## The problem

For most service businesses, referrals are the best channel and the least managed one. The [Referral Request Scripts](/toolbox/referral-request-scripts) write one good ask, and then the system ends: nobody tracks who was asked, who said "of course!" and never sent anyone, which thank-you went out, or that your best referrer has now sent three clients and received nothing but a text. Word of mouth runs on timing and reciprocity, and both need a memory. This skill installs it.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Three ways to use it:

- **Claude apps:** zip this folder and upload it in Settings under Skills (paid plans with code execution switched on). Claude then loads it when a request matches.
- **Claude Code:** copy the folder to `~/.claude/skills/referral-program-copilot/`, or to `.claude/skills/` inside a project.
- **ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or Claude on the free plan:** paste the contents of `SKILL.md` at the start of a conversation or into a project's instructions.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, anything that accepts pasted text).
3. Do the setup, then seed the log with the referrals you can remember from the last year.
4. Report in two lines as things happen ("asked Dana, she said yes", "new client via Marcus").
5. Once a month, ask for the ask-ready list and send what it drafts, edited to taste.

## Example

A bookkeeping practice seeds the skill with its client list and last year's four remembered referrals. Month one, the ask-ready list has three names, each with a reason ("year-end cleanup praised two weeks ago, never asked"). Two asks go out, one client says yes. Month two's report includes "new client signed, came via Priya," and the skill answers with the log updated and a flag: Priya has now referred twice and the thank-you ledger shows nothing sent, with a suggested gesture matched to the setup answers. By month four the pattern note reads: three of five referrals are restaurant owners; the skill suggests the next round of asks lead with the two restaurant clients not yet asked, and drafts a blurb that names the niche.

## Pro tip

Seed the log honestly, including the asks that went nowhere; the skill's "one graceful nudge, then stop" rule only protects relationships if it knows who was already asked. And when the pattern read names a niche, act on it in your marketing too; a referral concentration is your market telling you where you already have a reputation, which is intelligence worth more than the referral itself.
