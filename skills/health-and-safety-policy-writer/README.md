# Health and Safety Policy Writer

> The written health and safety policy, the violence and harassment policies and the posting checklist an Ontario workplace with six or more workers has to have, drafted from how your shop runs

**Category:** HR & Hiring · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude

## The problem

Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act says an employer with more than five workers regularly employed must have a written health and safety policy, review it at least once a year, run a program to carry it out, and post it where workers will see it. Every workplace also needs workplace violence and workplace harassment policies, written down once you're past five workers. Most small businesses find this out from a Ministry inspector, or from the form that asks for the policy after someone gets hurt. The policy itself is a page. The program behind it is the work. This skill drafts both from how your workplace runs, and it gives you the posting and review calendar so the page doesn't go stale on the wall.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, anything that accepts pasted text).
3. Answer the interview: the work, the hazards, the headcount, the sites, the training you already run.
4. Review the drafted policies with whoever will sign them, adjust the responsibilities to real names and roles, and post them.
5. Put the review date in your calendar and log training as it happens.

## Example

A twelve-person auto body shop in London, Ontario has a safety binder from 2019 that nobody has opened since. The skill's interview turns up spray-booth chemicals, lifts, a customer-facing counter and a Saturday shift where one person works alone. The drafted policy statement fits on a page. The harassment policy names the owner as the first contact and the shop's accountant as the alternate, so a complaint about the owner has somewhere to go. The program outline assigns the lift inspections to the senior technician and the WHMIS binder to the office manager. The posting checklist reveals the shop has no health and safety representative, which it needed at six workers. The owner holds the selection that Friday, posts the four documents by the time clock, and sets the review for next October.

## Pro tip

Date and sign the policy by hand every year, even when nothing changed. An inspector reads the date before the words. And when you have a lone worker, write the check-in rule into the violence policy, not into a text message thread: it's the kind of thing that only matters once, and that once is when the document counts.
