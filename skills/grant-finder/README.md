# Grant Finder

> A shortlist of grants you might qualify for, with draft answers

**Category:** Finance & Admin · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

Grant money for small businesses exists, but finding it is a part-time job nobody has. Program pages are written in government prose, eligibility rules hide in PDFs, and half the lists floating around online are outdated or wrong. Worse, asking a bare AI chat "what grants can I get?" produces confident nonsense: program names that sound plausible and deadlines that were never real. Chasing one of those costs you an afternoon and some faith.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. Answer the skill's setup questions about your business.
4. Gather candidate programs from official sources (in Canada, start at the Canada Business Benefits Finder on canada.ca and your provincial equivalent) and paste each program page in.
5. Ask for the shortlist, walk the eligibility checklist for your top pick, then have it draft answers to that application's actual questions.
6. Before submitting anything, open the official page yourself and confirm eligibility and the deadline.

## Example

Say you run a 12-person food manufacturer in Nova Scotia. You paste in three program pages you found through official directories. Output (condensed): "Shortlist: Program A ranks first: the page states it funds equipment for food processors of your size in your province, and you meet 6 of 7 listed criteria. The seventh (two years of financial statements) needs checking with your accountant. Program B is a weaker fit: its page limits applicants to exporters, which you are not yet. Program C's page shows an intake window that may have closed; the page I saw gave a spring date without a year. Eligibility checklist for Program A follows. Verify eligibility and all deadlines on the official program page before applying: programs change and pages go stale."

## Pro tip

Paste the application's actual questions, not only the program overview page. Draft answers written against the real questions, in the program's own vocabulary, are where this skill saves you the most time, and it will flag any question it cannot answer from your setup facts instead of padding.
