# Content Repurposing Studio

> One blog post becomes ten pieces of content in your voice

**Category:** Marketing & Content · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

You wrote one good blog post this month. It deserved a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a newsletter mention, and a short video script, and it got none of them, because each format is its own twenty-minute job and the AI drafts you tried sounded like a press release. Repurposing by copy-paste is worse: a blog intro pasted onto LinkedIn reads exactly like a blog intro pasted onto LinkedIn.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. In setup, paste 3 to 5 pieces of your best writing so it can learn your voice from evidence.
4. Paste one finished source piece and ask for the full spread, or name the two formats you need this week.
5. Edit the drafts lightly, post them over the following two weeks, and tell the skill which ones landed.

## Example

Say you run a bookkeeping practice and you wrote a post titled "Why your profit and your bank balance disagree." You paste it in. Output (condensed): a LinkedIn post opening "Your books say you made $8,000 last month. Your bank account says otherwise. Both are right."; a 7-tweet thread walking the timing gap with the invoice example from your post; a newsletter blurb ("The question every client asks in March, answered in four minutes"); a 60-second video script starting on the same $8,000 line with a note on where to pause; and three pull quotes, including the one line from your post that works out of context. Plus one flag: "Your post mentions a client story in passing. I did not expand it, since the details aren't in the source. Add two sentences there and the thread gets stronger."

## Pro tip

Feed it your worst-performing repurposed post along with your best one during setup, and say which is which. Knowing what flopped in your niche sharpens the voice profile faster than five more good samples, because it learns what to avoid, not only what to copy.
