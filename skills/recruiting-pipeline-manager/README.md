# Recruiting Pipeline Manager

> Every candidate moved, messaged, and scored from one place

**Category:** HR & Hiring · **Difficulty:** Light setup · **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code

## The problem

Hiring without an applicant tracking system means hiring out of an inbox. Candidates sit unanswered for two weeks, two interviewers ask different questions and compare gut feelings, someone promising gets forgotten in a folder, and the rejection emails never go out at all. The spreadsheet you started with good intentions died at row nine. None of this is a character flaw; it is what happens when a pipeline lives in nobody's head officially.

## The skill

The installable file is [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) in this folder. Add it to your assistant's skills folder, or paste it at the start of a conversation.

## How to use it

1. Click "Install skill" on this page and copy the install prompt.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor. Anything that can fetch a URL or accept pasted text).
3. Answer the skill's setup questions about the role and your stages.
4. Paste your current candidate list, however messy, and let it build the pipeline and flag missing information.
5. After anything happens (application, interview, silence), tell the skill in a sentence and let it update stages and draft the next messages.
6. Ask for the weekly summary every Monday and clear the "needs you" list first.

## Example

Say you run a 14-person landscaping company hiring two crew leads. You paste 19 names from your inbox and job board. Output (condensed): "Pipeline built: 19 candidates. Applied: 11, Screening: 5, Interview: 2, Stalled: 1 (Jordan, interviewed 12 days ago, no decision recorded: this is your oldest debt). Drafts ready: 5 screening invitations, 6 rejections for candidates who don't meet the licence requirement you set (each states the reason plainly and thanks them; review before sending, a human approves every rejection). Scorecards: both interviewed candidates scored on your rubric; Priya 22/30 with evidence cited, Sam 17/30 with two criteria marked 'no evidence, ask in follow-up.' Reminder: your provinces' employment rules differ on probation and overtime for supervisors; have an employment lawyer check the offer terms before anything is signed."

## Pro tip

Tell the skill about silence, not only about events. "No reply from anyone this week" is data: it triggers the follow-up drafts and stall flags that keep candidates from quietly evaporating, which is where inbox hiring loses most of its best people.
